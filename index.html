Opening Roth conversion history

Healthcare

Insurance until Medicare
20 controls

Confirm tax-dependent status, employer coverage affordability/minimum value, Medicaid/CHIP and other coverage eligibility before choosing eligible. The MFS exception must meet IRS requirements; living apart alone does not establish it.

Member coverage calendar

Use primary, spouse, or a dependent name. Eligibility is eligible, employer, medicaid, dependent (claimed by another tax family), or unknown. Only eligible months receive a modeled credit. Enter Marketplace premiums in today’s dollars, before tax credits, without thousands separators. All members and months need their own records; unspecified future years receive no subsidy until confirmed. A dependent you claim can be eligible. The calendar overrides household premium quotes for its specified years.

Assumption: ACA subsidy math uses coverage-year household size, marketplace member ages, and prior-year FPL guidelines for the selected tax year.

Plan & premium overrides

Assumption: exact plan mode calculates PTC from the household SLCSP and applies the selected plan gross premium and OOP max. Quoted net premium mode trusts the quote and bypasses PTC recalculation.

Backup plan (high-MAGI fallback)

Assumption: the backup plan becomes active only when modeled ACA MAGI exceeds the trigger, so it can model subsidy cliffs without replacing low-MAGI coverage.

Federal Marketplace plan finder

Assumption: federal Marketplace lookup uses the HealthCare.gov API for FFM states. State-based exchanges are routed to localized benchmarks offline, or accept manual local plan overrides.

Other income

Social Security, work, SE
24+ controls

For benefits already received, enter the actual claim age and current annual award. For an unclaimed benefit, enter the estimate at the selected future age. Past elections are locked; claim withdrawal, voluntary suspension, retroactive awards, and exact claim-month cash flows are not modeled.

Claim-month and earnings-test detail

The annual earnings test is automatic before full retirement age. To use the special first-year monthly rule, enter all 12 months and reconcile their total to annual wages plus net self-employment income. Otherwise the annual test applies. In the FRA year, missing monthly detail conservatively treats all annual earnings as pre-FRA earnings. Withheld months adjust future worker benefits at FRA; auxiliary/survivor adjustments require SSA review.

Recurring income streams

Pensions, annuities, rent, and other recurring income: age-started on the owner's clock, optional COLA, survivor share after the owner's death, and state retirement-income treatment for pensions/annuities.

Strategy toolkit

Taxes, allocations, withdrawal rules
43 controls

Assumption: discretionary spending is 100% when stocks are less than 10% below prior highs, 50% from 10% to 20%, and 0% beyond 20%; essential spending inflates, discretionary spending stays nominal.

Risk-based guardrails solve a fixed failsafe spend, starting spend, lower cut trigger, and upper raise trigger from the selected historical backtest cohorts.

HSA eligibility and expense history

Starting expense balance is the documented receipt total before the reimbursements/deductions entered here. Only expenses incurred after the HSA was established qualify. ACA premiums and Medigap are excluded by default; enter only a supported insurance exception such as COBRA or coverage while receiving unemployment compensation. Qualified out-of-pocket costs accrue to the receipt pool. Medicare B/D premiums accrue only when all living adults are 65+; mixed-age receipt ownership requires review. Contributions assume eligible HDHP coverage, no disqualifying coverage, and no Medicare enrollment; the model stops contributions at 65. Each spouse’s $1,000 age-55 catch-up goes into their own account and is not inflation-indexed. The last-month rule is not assumed.

Shared allocation target

Survivor budgets

Enter separate budgets in today's dollars for each surviving spouse. These replace the household spending strategy after the year of death and grow with inflation. Include healthcare and taxes only when your target-spend settings include them.

Legacy / heir assumptions

Assumption: automated tax moves are capped by bracket, ACA, NIIT, IRMAA, and liquidity guardrails before they are treated as recommended actions.
